**Project Name:** Songwriting Prompts

## Program Description
Songwriting Prompts offers text messaging services to provide daily songwriting prompts via SMS and on-demand reports via email to users who have opted in.

## User Consent (Opt-In)
By signing up or providing your mobile phone number via a text message to the service, you expressly consent to receive recurring text messages from Songwriting Prompts. Consent is not a condition of any purchase or service.

## Message Frequency
Message frequency varies based on user activity. You will receive at least 1 message per day.

## Cost and Rates
Message and data rates may apply. Check with your mobile carrier for details on your specific pricing plan.

## Opt-Out Instructions (STOP)
To stop receiving text messages from us, reply STOP to any message you receive from Songwriting Prompts. You will receive a one-time opt-out confirmation text message, and no further messages will be sent to your number unless you re-enroll.

## Help Instructions (HELP)
For help or support regarding our text messaging program, reply HELP to any message, or contact us at ryanhartkopfmusic@gmail.com

Mobile carriers supported include AT&T, Verizon, T-Mobile, Sprint, and others. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

We respect your privacy. We will not share or sell your mobile number or personal data to third parties for marketing purposes. For more information, please review our [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md).
