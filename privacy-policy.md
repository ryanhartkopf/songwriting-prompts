# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** 2026-07-28
**Project Name:** Songwriting Prompts ("We," "Our," or "Us")  

## Introduction
Songwriting Prompts respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you opt into our SMS messaging program.

## Information We Collect
We collect personal information that you provide to us directly, which includes:
* Name
* Mobile phone number
* Email address
* Any messages or content you send via text

## How We Obtain Consent
We collect your mobile phone number only when you explicitly provide it by texting us directly. By opting in to our SMS messaging program, you consent to receive text messages from us. You can withdraw your consent at any time by replying **STOP** to any message you receive.

## How We Use Your Information
We use the information we collect to:
* Provide customer support and respond to inquiries
* Send transactional or operational text messages (such as service notifications)
* Send you email summaries of your text message activity
* Improve our services and communication

## Sharing of Information
**No personal information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes.** All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties. 

We may share your data only with service providers who help us run our communications or as required by law.

## Message Terms & Conditions
* At least one SMS message (songwriting prompt) will be sent per day, but you may receive more or fewer messages depending on your engagement with our services.
* Message and data rates may apply.
* You can reply **STOP** at any time to cancel or opt out of future messages. 
* You can reply **HELP** for assistance or information.

## Your Data Rights & Opting Out
If you wish to stop receiving text messages, reply **STOP** to any message you receive from us. You may also contact us to update or request the deletion of your personal data.

## Changes to This Policy
We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will post the updated policy on this page with a new effective date.
